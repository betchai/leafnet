# Phase 9 Status — Research Insights, Analytics & Model Intelligence

**Date:** 2026-08-24
**No model retrained. No dataset/test-set modification. No fabricated insights.**

---

## 1. Objective
Transform recorded data (dataset records, Phase-6 evaluations, application predictions) into traceable, evidence-based insights with a dedicated dashboard, API, and reports.

## 2. Insights implemented
- **Dataset insights:** counts, percentages, splits, largest/smallest present class, imbalance ratio, shortfalls vs 500/class
- **Performance insights:** accuracy, per-class P/R/F1/support, macro/weighted; best/weakest by named metric; statistical warning when n<30
- **Confusion insights:** directional ranked pairs, total misclassified, no causal inference
- **Confidence/error insights:** high-confidence errors (≥0.8 configurable), low-confidence set, correct-vs-incorrect mean comparison with honest direction reporting
- **Improvement opportunities:** every recommendation cites its evidence
